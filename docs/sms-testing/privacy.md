# Privacy Policy

Ivan Malison — EVA Testing is a private SMS development and testing program
operated by Ivan Malison. This policy applies only to this testing program,
not to every feature or installation of the EVA application.

The program processes the participant's phone number, SMS consent and opt-out
records, message contents, delivery status, and technical test logs to send
requested test messages, diagnose delivery, and honor messaging preferences.
Twilio and mobile carriers process this information to deliver the messages.
Test records remain in the operator's development systems and Twilio account
until removed; this program does not promise a fixed deletion period.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third
parties for marketing purposes.

Participation is currently limited to the operator's own phone. Do not send
passwords, authentication codes, payment details, or other sensitive information
as test content. Reply STOP to stop messages. Contact IvanMalison@gmail.com
with privacy questions or requests to delete test records.
